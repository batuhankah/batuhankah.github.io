Lumo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Lumo has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your game progress is stored on your phone. We use Google AdMob to show ads and Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone) to process purchases; these services process some device data under their own policies. You can turn off personalized ads at any time and delete all game data with the Delete my data link at the bottom of the main menu.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile game Lumo (the "Game"), developed by Batuhan Kahraman (Lumo Lightworks) ("we", "us"). We are the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and, for players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

Contact: batuhankah0@gmail.com · Address: Cumhuriyet Mah., Zerdali Sk. No:3, 34000 Küçükçekmece / Istanbul, Türkiye

2. What data we process

2.1 Game data stored on your device

The Game stores the following only on your own device, in the Game's local storage. This data is not sent to us:

  • Game progress: unlocked sectors, best results, star dust balance, upgrades, weapons and outfits
  • Achievements, daily quests, Light Pass progress, story chapters you have seen
  • Settings: sound volumes, vibration, screen shake, difficulty
  • A record of purchased items (which item was bought, not payment details)

This data does not identify you. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or tap Delete my data.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show ads in the Game. Watching an ad for a reward is always optional; an interstitial ad may also be shown between some runs. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may process:

  • The advertising ID (Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level)
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system, language and network type
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, views)

This data goes directly to Google; we do not access it or link it to individual players. For how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

2.3 Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store)

In-game purchases are made through Google Play Billing on Android and the Apple App Store on iPhone. Your payment details (such as card numbers) are processed by Google or Apple; we never see or store them. Google only sends us the information needed to confirm which item was bought and whether the purchase is valid (an order/purchase token).

2.4 Game statistics and error reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the Game we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They process:

  • Game events: starting and finishing a sector (win/loss, room reached, duration, difficulty), completing the tutorial, watching an ad, tapping a shop button and similar in-game steps
  • Technical information: device model, operating system version, language, country, app version and a randomly generated app installation ID
  • Error reports: a technical error log when the Game crashes or hits an error

Advertising ID collection is turned off in Firebase Analytics, and this data is not used to personalize ads. We look at it as aggregate statistics (e.g. "40% of players reached sector 2"); it is not used to identify anyone individually. Details: Firebase privacy and security.

2.5 Data we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise (GPS) location. The Game has no accounts and no chat.

4. Ad choices and consent

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent dialog when the Game is first opened and can accept or refuse personalized ads. In countries where no consent dialog is shown (including Türkiye), no ad consent is collected from you, so the Game requests only non-personalized ads from Google; these ads are not chosen based on your interests.

  • You can change your choice at any time with the Ad preferences link at the bottom of the Game's main menu.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads.
  • On iPhone, Apple's tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) prompt appears when you first open the game. If you don't allow it, your advertising ID (IDFA) is not shared and ads are not personalized; the game works the same. You can change this under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • The No Ads purchase removes interstitial ads; rewarded ads always remain optional.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Only the following service providers process data, as needed to run the Game:

  • Google LLC – AdMob (advertising), Google Play (distribution, payments, ratings), Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (game statistics, error reports)
  • Apple Inc. – App Store (distribution, payments and ratings for the iPhone version). Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Google's servers are located abroad (mainly in the United States), so data processed for ads and payments is transferred outside Türkiye and the EEA. This transfer takes place under KVKK Art. 9 and Chapter V of the GDPR, relying on the data protection commitments and standard contractual clauses in Google's terms of service. You can email us with any questions about international transfers.

6. How long we keep data

  • Game data on your device: until you uninstall the Game or delete it.
  • Advertising data: subject to Google's retention periods.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple as required by law.

7. Children

Lumo is intended for players aged 13 and over; it is not directed at children under 13. Ads shown in the Game are limited to the "Teen" content rating at most. If we learn that we have processed data of a child under 13, we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its processing, and claim compensation for damage. Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland have similar rights under the GDPR, including the right to withdraw consent at any time and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

  • To delete game data: Use the Delete my data link at the bottom of the Game's main menu. This cannot be undone. Purchased items are linked to your Google account and can be brought back with Restore.
  • For other requests: write to batuhankah0@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days at the latest.
  • You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Game data is kept only on your device, so your device's security (screen lock, up-to-date system) matters for protecting it. Ad and payment communication uses Google's encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will announce important changes in the Game or on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version.
